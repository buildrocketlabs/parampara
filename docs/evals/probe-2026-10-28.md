# Natural typed questions on the live site · 28 Oct 2026

**Why.** A jury walk on 28 Oct typed a question the way a teacher would ("…cannot read. … cries when I ask him to read") and got "no master has answered this yet". The sample chips are worded to meet the library; real teachers are not. This probe asks what real typing gets.

**Method.** `tests/e2e/probe.spec.ts` (Playwright project `probe`) against https://parampara-eta.vercel.app, run 27 Oct 22:16 UTC (28 Oct, 03:46 IST), as the one tap Sample teacher on an emulated Pixel 7, typing 15 fresh questions: feelings first, reluctant readers, absence, several classes in one room, number sense, writing, parents and behaviour, in English, Kannada and both mixed, always "a child", never a name. For an answered question it opens the trace ("What we understood": topic · class · intent; "Practice cards we found": how many the ranker considered and chose). A "no card" outcome shows the teacher no trace, so its topic and class were read where triage left them, in `/r/queue` as the one tap Sample reviewer. Which cards were candidates is worked out from those with the app's own deterministic prefilter (`prefilter` in `lib/ask/pipeline.ts`: same topic, or same subject and an overlapping class, else the whole index). Raw rows: `docs/evals/probe/2026-10-27T22-16-22.json`.

**Spend.** 15 fresh questions (the cap). 5 answered and 2 checked to the end at about 3 to 4 US cents each, 8 stopped after triage and ranking at about 1 cent each: about **$0.30 to $0.35** of model time, under the $0.45 allowance. The drawer's total did not parse in this run, so this is the estimate from `docs/evals/latency.md`, not a sum of traces.

Every rule in `lib/seed/library-plan.test.ts` holds: the deterministic safety patterns (no punishment, shaming, fear, diagnosis; not even naming a harm to forbid it), both languages on every field, three to five steps and a reason, no em dashes, no person named, distinct titles and problems, stable ids. The Sample library goes from 60 to 68 approved cards. Parents and home language stay gaps on purpose.

p05 and p06 did find cards; their answers fell short at the grounding check, which is the guardrail working (an unsupported step is removed, and too few left goes to a person). Cards 63 and 59 give them closer practice to cite; whether that is enough shows only on a re-run.
