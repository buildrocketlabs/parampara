## Rules
1. Consent before recording, every time, in the speaker's language, recorded in the app (or on the phone recorder, then imported).
2. No children's faces or names anywhere. Classroom photos only from behind or of hands, slates, blackboards, with the teacher's and headmaster's permission.
3. Never commit real recordings, transcripts, phone numbers or names to the repo. Field data lives in Supabase (private) and a local folder outside git.
4. Teachers are never ranked or reported on by name.
5. Write down what went wrong too. Judges trust honest numbers.
