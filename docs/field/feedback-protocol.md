# Feedback protocol

## Watched sessions (5 to 10 teachers at Talkal, Mon 26 Oct)
Hand the phone, say only "ask any question about your class". Do not help. Note: first tap, hesitation over 3 s, words they use, whether they find the answer's master, whether they hear it, whether they share the card, and the next day whether they answer *Did yesterday's idea work?*. Ten minutes, then three questions:
1. ಇದು ನಿಮಗೆ ಉಪಯೋಗವಾಯಿತೇ? (Was it useful?)
2. ಯಾವುದು ಗೊಂದಲ ಉಂಟುಮಾಡಿತು? (What confused you?)
3. ಇನ್ನೊಬ್ಬ ಶಿಕ್ಷಕರಿಗೆ ಹೇಳುತ್ತೀರಾ? (Would you tell another teacher?)

## Field week (30 to 50 teachers by invite link, Tue 27 to Thu 29 Oct)
- Daily: read the human queue and unsupported answers; fix the top issue the same day; log it.
- Day 3: reminder message.
- Day 5: 10 short calls (5 min) using the same three questions. Record quotes with permission (first name and district only).

## Master panel (50 answers)
Each master rates answers drawn from real questions: *I would say this too* / *partly* / *I would not say this*, plus one line why. Results feed `docs/evals/latest.md` honestly.
