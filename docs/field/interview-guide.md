# Master interview guide (30 to 40 minutes, Kannada)

The interviewer role in the app follows this guide; a human interviewer can follow it too. Ask for **one real story per topic**: what the child did, what the teacher did, step by step, and what changed. Avoid theory.

Warm up (2 min): years of teaching, which classes, which school, a child they still remember (no name).

| # | Topic (FLN) | Opening question | Probes |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Letter sounds, ಅಕ್ಷರ ಗುರುತು | A child in Class 1 can't recognise letters after months. What did you do? | Materials used? How many days? How did you know it worked? |
| 2 | Blending and reading words, ಓದು | A child knows letters but can't read words. | Which words first? Games? Pair reading? |
| 3 | Number sense, ಸಂಖ್ಯೆ | A child counts by rote but doesn't understand quantity. | Stones, sticks, bundles? Local objects? |
| 4 | Multi level class | Class 1 and 2 together, 40 children, one teacher. | How did you group? What did others do meanwhile? |
| 5 | Attendance and interest | A child stops coming or sits silently. | Home visit? Parents? First small win? |
| 6 | Home language vs Kannada | Children speak Lambani, Urdu, Marathi or a strong dialect at home. | Bridging words? Songs? |
| 7 | Parents and community | Parents can't read; how did you involve them? | Melas, homework without reading, SDMC |

Close (2 min): "If a young teacher remembers only one thing from you, what should it be?" Record this as the master's signature line.

Interviewer rules: never correct the master; ask "what did you do next?" up to three times; if a child's name comes up, gently say "let us call the child *ondu magu*"; if they describe punishment, do not extract it as a practice (the extractor drops it).
