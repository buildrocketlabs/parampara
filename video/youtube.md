## Description

A headmaster's 32 years of classroom wisdom, in every young teacher's pocket, by voice, in Kannada.

Mahesh Bedavatti, Headmaster of Government Higher Primary School, Ambedkar Nagar, Talakal (Koppal), sent seven WhatsApp voice notes. Parampara turned them into six practice cards, each step tied to the second he said it. A young teacher at 9 pm asks in Kannada and hears what to try tomorrow, from a real master, by name.

Try it: https://parampara-eta.vercel.app
Code (open source): {public repo link}

How it works, one line per part of the loop:

- Capture: a master teacher talks, or sends voice notes; Claude drafts practice cards that cite the exact seconds of the recording.
- Check: a headmaster approves every card, with the master's own audio beside each step. Nothing reaches a teacher unchecked.
- Deliver: a teacher asks by voice or text; the answer uses only approved cards, a second model checks every step, and it is spoken in Kannada (or Hindi), with the master's name and his own voice.
- Learn: she taps "it worked", and the master hears that his wisdom helped.
- Spread: officers see what teachers struggle with each week, anonymously, without ever watching a teacher.
- Trust: grounded or silent; children's names removed before anything is stored; Childline 1098 for a child at risk; works on slow networks and offline; about ₹33 a teacher a month.

Built for the AI for Foundational Learning Hackathon (ShikshaNext, Central Square Foundation, Anthropic), Challenge 3: Teacher Support and Training.
